Use HTML to help me understand what we're discussing. Show me with big pictures and little words without witholding key details. 
Use your own taste, avoid lazy design, integrate real-world pictures/icons/visuals as needed but only selectively when it makes sense.
When you do use prose/detail: Be clear, concise/terse, and avoid jargon. Explain things as if the viewer is new here.
